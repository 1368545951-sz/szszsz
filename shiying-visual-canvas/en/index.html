INTERACTION NOTES

How to use

  1. Drag an empty area to pan. Scroll, or use the controls at the bottom right, to zoom.
  2. Drag a photo to the reference area below. Related photos stand out; other photos stay on the canvas.
  3. Open a photo and drag one of its four handles to use its color, background, subject or composition. You can also click a handle.
  4. Use ＋ / − on a reference to find more or less similar features. Use × to remove a clue. Click a text clue to change its importance.
  5. Space and Sequence change the layout without clearing your clues.

This is an interaction prototype with 118 photos. It uses manually assigned visual tags, not live AI. The target photo receives no special ranking boost. Fading does not delete a photo, and selecting photos does not change the original files. Accuracy and performance with larger libraries have not been tested.
